About the bootcamp

The Genoa bootcamp brings three organizations together on one programme: PALNET, the Safe Brain Initiative, and SAFETOTS. It is the first PALNET workshop in Europe, and follows the format that filled both San Diego sessions within weeks.

PALNET's contribution is the hands-on half: small groups of five clinicians work with a PALNET teacher, reading EEG from an on-screen video library of real pediatric cases and from an EEG simulator of simulated cases, displayed on BIS Advance and Sedline monitors. Unlike EEG sessions built purely around lectures, attendees spend the majority of the time reading EEG themselves.

Who should attend

Pediatric anesthesiologists, fellows, residents, and allied anesthesia professionals who want to use EEG in daily practice. No prior EEG experience is assumed — the session begins with level-setting lectures before moving to hands-on interpretation.
