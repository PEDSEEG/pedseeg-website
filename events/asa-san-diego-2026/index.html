Getting there

Where. UC San Diego, Seventh College, West Building 1, 15th Floor Meeting Room (15A), 10176 Scholars Dr N, La Jolla, CA 92093. Go in the front doors closest to Scholars Drive North and take the elevators to the 15th floor.

When to arrive. Saturday 12:30 to 12:45 PM for a 1:00 PM start. Sunday 7:30 to 7:45 AM for an 8:00 AM start.

From the convention center. UC San Diego is 20 to 30 minutes north of downtown by car. A rideshare is the easiest way; use 10176 Scholars Dr N, La Jolla, CA 92093 as the address. If you'd like to share a ride with others from your session, email us and we'll put you in touch. About the workshop

PALNET's workshop brings together members and potential PALNET members from North and South America, Europe, and Asia for a half-day of hands-on EEG-guided pediatric anesthesia training, offered in two time slots to help accommodate your schedule.

The training sessions use a video library of EEG from actual cases, and an EEG simulator of pretend cases viewed on the BIS Advance or Sedline device. PALNET teachers lead small-group instruction at the sessions. Each attendee has a table assignment for the sessions, and every attendee leaves with a PALNET certificate documenting their training.

The workshop is led by PALNET teachers:

  • C. Dean Kurth, MD — Children's Hospital of Philadelphia
